Setting up your profile takes a little less than five minutes.

This is the page you land on when you click your profile icon in the top right corner and select **Profile**. From here you can update how you appear to teammates, add a way for them to reach you directly, and decide how Heyy notifies you when new messages come in.

<Frame>
  ![Profile page showing profile picture, name fields, phone number, and message notifications toggle](/assets/profile-general.png)
</Frame>

## Set your name

Fill in your **First name** and **Last name**. This is what shows up across your workspace, so use whatever you'd want your team to see in chats, mentions, and notifications.

## Add a profile picture

Click the upload icon under **Profile picture** and pick a photo from your device. No need for a formal headshot, just something that helps you and your teammates in the same workspace recognize your account at a glance.

## Add your phone number (optional)

Add your number in the **Phone number** field. This is optional, and it's only visible to your workspace, never to your contacts.

## Turn on message notifications

Flip the **Message notifications** toggle on to get notified in-app the moment a new message comes in. Leave it off if you'd rather check messages on your own manually.

<Tip>
  Once your profile is set up, head to [Security](/help/managing-your-profile/security) to update your email and turn on two-factor authentication.
</Tip>
