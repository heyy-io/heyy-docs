Update sign-in and security options for your personal Heyy account (not workspace-wide team settings).

## Open security settings

1. Click your profile menu in the app header.
2. Open [**Profile → Security**](https://app.heyy.io/profile/security).

Use this page to change your password and review account security options available on your plan.

<Note>
  Workspace settings such as team members and billing live under [**Settings**](https://app.heyy.io/settings) — see [Workspace roles](/help/managing-your-workspace/workspace-roles) and [Invite teammates](/help/managing-your-workspace/workspace-members/how-to-invite-teammates).
</Note>
