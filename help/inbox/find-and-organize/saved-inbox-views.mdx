---
title: "Saved inbox views"
description: "Save filtered views of your chat list in the Inbox."
---

**Inbox views** (saved chat views) filter which conversations appear in your [**Inbox**](https://app.heyy.io/inbox) — for example open chats, a label, or a team assignment.

## Create or edit an inbox view

1. Open [**Inbox**](https://app.heyy.io/inbox).
2. Use the views section in the sidebar to add or select a saved view.
3. Configure filters for chats (not contact-table filters).

Saved inbox views use the `chat` view type in Heyy. Your plan may limit how many saved inbox views you can create.

<Note>
  Heyy also has **contact views** for the Contacts mini-CRM. See [Saved contact views](/help/contacts/search-and-filters/saved-contact-views).
</Note>
