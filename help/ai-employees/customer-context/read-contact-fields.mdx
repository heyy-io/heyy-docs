---
title: "Let your employee read contact fields"
description: "Grant read access to contact data in chat."
---

On **Attributes** (v2 employees only):

1. Click **Add attribute**.
2. Choose a built-in or custom [workspace attribute](/help/contacts/mini-crm/custom-attributes-as-columns).
3. Enable **Read**.

The employee can use these values in replies and [guideline mentions](/help/ai-employees/identity-and-guidelines/use-mentions-in-guidelines). Only expose fields the AI truly needs — fewer fields mean fewer mistakes.
