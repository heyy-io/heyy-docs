---
title: "Set boundaries and escalation"
description: "Limit what the AI may say or do."
---

**Boundaries** guidelines define hard limits: no sensitive data, no guaranteed outcomes, staying on topic, declining harmful requests, and [escalating when unsure](/help/ai-employees/going-live/hand-off-to-human).

Add explicit instructions for when to offer a human teammate. Pair boundary rules with [exit conditions](/help/ai-employees/going-live/exit-conditions) on the automation **AI Employee** action.
