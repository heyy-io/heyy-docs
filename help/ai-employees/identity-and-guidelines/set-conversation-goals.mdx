---
title: "Set conversation goals"
description: "Guide what the AI should achieve in each chat."
---

Use **Objectives** guidelines on **Identity & guidelines** to define outcomes — for example, resolve on first reply, qualify before handoff, or propose clear next steps.

Goals should describe what the *employee* tries to do in the thread, not personal goals for your human team. Combine with [boundaries](/help/ai-employees/identity-and-guidelines/set-boundaries-and-escalation) so the model knows when to stop or escalate.
