---
title: "Use @mentions in guidelines"
description: "Reference attributes, content, and actions in prompts."
---

In the guideline editor, type `@` to insert:

- **Attributes** — Contact fields the employee can read or update
- **Content** — Knowledge sources from the Content tab
- **Actions** — Tools the employee may invoke

Mentions keep guidelines aligned with what you actually configured. If nothing appears in the menu, add [contact context](/help/ai-employees/customer-context/choose-attributes-to-expose), [knowledge](/help/ai-employees/content-and-actions/add-your-website), or [actions](/help/ai-employees/content-and-actions/when-the-ai-can-take-action) first.
