---
title: "Write conversation guidelines"
description: "Add structured instructions by category."
---

Guidelines are prompts injected into your employee's system instructions (v2). They are grouped into four categories:

| Category | Purpose |
| -------- | ------- |
| Identity & style | Who they are and how they communicate |
| Objectives | What to achieve in each conversation |
| Knowledge base & tools | When to use content and actions |
| Boundaries | What they must not do |

To add a guideline:

1. Open **Identity & guidelines**.
2. Expand a category accordion.
3. Click **Add guideline** and fill in title, status, and prompt.

Use [mentions](/help/ai-employees/identity-and-guidelines/use-mentions-in-guidelines) to reference contact fields, knowledge, and actions. Try built-in **suggestions** when creating a guideline.
