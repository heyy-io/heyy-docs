---
title: "Define your brand voice and role"
description: "Set name, brand, role, and identity for an AI Employee."
---

In [**AI Employees → Identity & guidelines**](https://app.heyy.io/ai-employees):

1. Select an employee.
2. Under **Basics**, set:
   - **Name** — How the employee is labeled in Heyy.
   - **Brand name** — Customer-facing brand they represent.
   - **Role** — Support, Sales, or General (shapes default behavior).

Role descriptions in the app explain each option. Pair this with [tone and answer length](/help/ai-employees/identity-and-guidelines/set-tone-and-answer-length) and [guidelines](/help/ai-employees/identity-and-guidelines/write-conversation-guidelines).
