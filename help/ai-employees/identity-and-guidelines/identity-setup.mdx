**Identity setup** is where you define who your AI employee is and how they sound before you add knowledge, capabilities, or deploy them in automations. These settings live on the **Identity & guidelines** tab in [**AI Employees**](https://app.heyy.io/ai-employees) and apply to every reply unless a [guideline](/help/ai-employees/identity-and-guidelines/write-your-guidelines) or [automation override](/help/ai-employees/going-live/automation-guideline-overrides) says otherwise.

Getting identity right early saves rework later. Role, tone, and answer length shape how customers *feel* the conversation; name, brand, and gender shape who they think they are talking to and how consistently the employee speaks — especially in gendered languages. You can always refine with guidelines — but defaults here set the baseline your team tests in the [Playground](/help/ai-employees/going-live/test-in-playground).

<Tip>
  Identity setup covers **Basics** and **Generation settings** only. Detailed behavior rules belong in [Write your guidelines](/help/ai-employees/identity-and-guidelines/write-your-guidelines) and the category articles that follow.
</Tip>

## How identity setup works

When a customer message reaches your AI employee, Heyy combines identity setup with guidelines, [content](/help/ai-employees/content/add-your-website), and [capabilities](/help/ai-employees/capabilities/choose-attributes-to-expose) to produce each reply.

**Basics** answer *who* the employee is — their name, the brand they represent, and their high-level role (support, sales, or general).

**Generation settings** answer *how* they communicate by default — tone of voice and how long answers tend to be.

Think of identity setup as the employee's profile card; [guidelines](/help/ai-employees/identity-and-guidelines/write-your-guidelines) are the playbook they follow in specific situations.

## Configure basics

Open an employee and scroll to **Basics** at the top of **Identity & guidelines**.

<Frame>
  ![Identity basics](/assets/ai-employees-identity-tab.png)
</Frame>

| Field | Description |
| ----- | ----------- |
| **Name** | The employee's actual name — how they introduce themselves in conversation. Use a real first name such as Jessica or Jimmy so replies feel human and stay consistent. |
| **Brand name** | The brand customers interact with in conversation. Use one employee per brand in multi-brand workspaces. |
| **Gender** | Sets the avatar (**Female**, **Male**, or **AI Employee**). Also keeps wording consistent in gendered languages such as Spanish, French, and Hebrew. |

**Role** shapes default behavior before you write guidelines. Each option shows a short description in the app when you select it.

<Frame>
  ![Role selection](/assets/ai-employees-identity-role.png)
</Frame>

| Role | Description |
| ---- | ----------- |
| **Support** | Handles questions and tickets. Best for FAQ, troubleshooting, order status, and routing issues to the right team. |
| **Sales** | Qualifies leads and books demos. Best for discovery questions, product fit, scheduling, and light objection handling. |
| **General** | Flexible all-purpose assistant. Best for mixed traffic, internal pilots, or channels where one employee handles many intents. |

You are not locked in — guidelines can narrow or override role defaults. Many teams start with **Support** or **General**, then add [Conversation flow & objectives](/help/ai-employees/identity-and-guidelines/conversation-flow-and-objectives) guidelines once they see real customer questions.

## Generation settings

**Tone of voice** and **answer length** set how replies feel and how much detail they include by default. Both apply to every message unless a guideline or [automation override](/help/ai-employees/going-live/automation-guideline-overrides) specifies otherwise.

<Frame>
  ![Tone of voice and answer length](/assets/ai-employees-identity-tone.png)
</Frame>

| Tone | Description |
| ---- | ----------- |
| **Professional** | B2B, regulated industries, or brands that prioritize clarity and formality. |
| **Neutral** | Balanced default — helpful without strong personality. |
| **Friendly** | Consumer brands, onboarding, and support where warmth builds trust. |
| **Playful** | Casual brands where humor and lightness match your marketing voice. |

| Answer length | Description |
| ------------- | ----------- |
| **Concise** | High-volume or mobile-first chats; quick, direct answers. |
| **Standard** | Most support and sales flows — enough context without overwhelming. |
| **Thorough** | Complex products, onboarding, or step-by-step guidance. |

Match tone to channel and audience — fintech WhatsApp often suits **Professional** or **Neutral**; D2C Instagram may suit **Friendly**. Pair **Concise** length with fast-moving channels like SMS. Pair **Thorough** length with [Content](/help/ai-employees/content/add-your-website) and [Q&A](/help/ai-employees/content/build-qa-knowledge-base) so longer answers stay accurate. If tone varies by channel, use [automation guideline overrides](/help/ai-employees/going-live/automation-guideline-overrides) instead of cloning employees.

## Identity setup vs guidelines

Identity setup and guidelines work together but serve different jobs.

| | Identity setup | Guidelines |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Purpose** | Global defaults for every reply | Structured rules by category |
| **Examples** | Role, tone, answer length | Escalation rules, when to use @Content, boundaries |
| **Where to configure** | Basics + Generation settings | Accordion categories on the same tab |

Defaults from this page are always active. Guidelines add nuance — for example, [Boundaries](/help/ai-employees/identity-and-guidelines/boundaries) for what not to say, or [Identity & style](/help/ai-employees/identity-and-guidelines/identity-and-style) for how to introduce the brand in the first message.
