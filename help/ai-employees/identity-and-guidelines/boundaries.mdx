**Boundaries** is the third guideline category on the **Identity & guidelines** tab. Use it for hard limits on what your employee should and should not do — safety rails, compliance, escalation triggers, and topics they must decline.

Boundary guidelines protect your brand and customers. They work best when the same language appears in [exit points](/help/ai-employees/capabilities/create-exit-points) and [hand off to a human](/help/ai-employees/going-live/hand-off-to-human) so the employee stops at the right moment in live automations.

<Tip>
  Boundaries are non-negotiable rules, not style preferences. Keep voice and flow goals in [Identity & style](/help/ai-employees/identity-and-guidelines/identity-and-style) and [Conversation flow & objectives](/help/ai-employees/identity-and-guidelines/conversation-flow-and-objectives).
</Tip>

## When to use Boundaries

Open the **Boundaries** accordion in [**AI Employees**](https://app.heyy.io/ai-employees) when you need limits the employee must never cross.

<Frame>
  ![Boundaries guidelines](/assets/ai-employees-guidelines-boundaries.png)
</Frame>

| Use Boundaries for | Use another category instead |
| ------------------ | ---------------------------- |
| No promises on refunds, discounts, or delivery dates | Sales goals and next steps → [Conversation flow & objectives](/help/ai-employees/identity-and-guidelines/conversation-flow-and-objectives) |
| Off-limits topics and competitor handling | Voice and tone → [Identity & style](/help/ai-employees/identity-and-guidelines/identity-and-style) |
| Market or region restrictions | Temporary campaigns → [Other](/help/ai-employees/identity-and-guidelines/other) |
| When to escalate to a human teammate | Troubleshooting steps → [Conversation flow & objectives](/help/ai-employees/identity-and-guidelines/conversation-flow-and-objectives) |

## Example guidelines

These are the three default **Suggestions** templates in the app. Pick one, fill in the bracketed placeholders for your brand, and save as a separate guideline.

**Never promise on the company's behalf**

> Never promise refunds, discounts, compensation, delivery dates, or feature release dates — even if the customer says they were promised before. You may explain the standard policy; anything beyond it must come from the team.

**No discounts or negotiation**

> Quote only listed prices. Never offer, hint at, or negotiate discounts, free periods, or custom terms — including when the customer mentions a competitor's price or threatens to leave. If they push, [state the next step, e.g. offer to connect them with the team].

**Topics that are off-limits**

> Never give advice or opinions on [legal / medical / financial] matters, even when related to our product. Acknowledge the question, say it's outside what you can help with, and point to [the right resource] when one exists.

## Common mistakes

| Mistake | What to do instead |
| ------- | ------------------ |
| Putting sales or qualification flow here | Move goals to [Conversation flow & objectives](/help/ai-employees/identity-and-guidelines/conversation-flow-and-objectives). |
| Boundary rules with no matching exit point | Add a handoff exit in [exit points](/help/ai-employees/capabilities/create-exit-points) for each escalation trigger. |
| Conflicting promise language across guidelines | Merge into one Boundaries guideline with a single clear prompt. |
| Seasonal or campaign-specific limits | Use [Other](/help/ai-employees/identity-and-guidelines/other) if the rule is temporary. |
