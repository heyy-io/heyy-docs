---
title: "Set tone and answer length"
description: "Control how concise and formal replies are."
---

On **Identity & guidelines**, configure **Generation settings**:

- **Tone of voice** — Professional, Neutral, Friendly, or Playful.
- **Answer length** — Concise, Standard, or Thorough.

These apply to every reply unless a [guideline](/help/ai-employees/identity-and-guidelines/write-conversation-guidelines) or [automation override](/help/ai-employees/going-live/automation-guideline-overrides) says otherwise. Test changes in the [Playground](/help/ai-employees/testing-and-quality/test-in-playground) before going live.
