---
title: "Add plain text knowledge"
description: "Paste policies, scripts, or notes as searchable content."
---

On the **Content** tab, open the **Text** section:

1. Click **Add text**.
2. Paste or type the content (policies, FAQs, internal notes).
3. Save.

Text knowledge is indexed like other content sources. Keep entries focused — shorter, well-scoped snippets retrieve more reliably than one giant block.
