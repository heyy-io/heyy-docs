---
title: "Keep knowledge accurate"
description: "Maintain content as products and policies change."
---

Review the **Content** tab when offers, policies, or docs change:

- **Update or remove** outdated links, documents, text, and Q&A entries.
- **Rename** sources so your team knows what each item covers.
- **Delete** sources that are no longer valid — stale content causes wrong answers.

After major edits, [test in the Playground](/help/ai-employees/going-live/test-in-playground) with the questions customers actually ask.
