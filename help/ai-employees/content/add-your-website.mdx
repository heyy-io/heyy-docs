---
title: "Add your website"
description: "Crawl links so your employee can answer from your site."
---

On the **Content** tab, open the **Links** section:

1. Click **Add link**.
2. Enter a URL (for example, your marketing site or help center).
3. Save — Heyy crawls and indexes the page for this employee.

Links stay internal to the employee; they are not sent to contacts. Add your most trusted public pages first, then expand. Reference links in [guidelines](/help/ai-employees/identity-and-guidelines/write-your-guidelines) with `@Content` when you want the employee to prefer certain sources.
