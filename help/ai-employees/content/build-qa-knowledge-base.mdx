---
title: "Build a Q&A knowledge base"
description: "Add question-and-answer pairs for precise replies."
---

On the **Content** tab, open the **Q&A** section:

1. Click **Add Q&A**.
2. Enter the question customers ask and the answer the employee should give.
3. Save.

Q&A pairs work well for pricing, hours, shipping, and other facts that should not drift. Combine with [links](/help/ai-employees/content/add-your-website) and [documents](/help/ai-employees/content/upload-documents) for broader coverage.
