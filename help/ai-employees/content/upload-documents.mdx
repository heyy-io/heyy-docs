---
title: "Upload documents"
description: "Add PDFs and files as internal knowledge."
---

On the **Content** tab, open the **Documents** section:

1. Click **Upload document**.
2. Select a file (PDF and other supported formats).
3. Wait for processing to finish — status appears on the item.

Documents are for **answers**, not for sending to contacts. Use [Share files in chat](/help/ai-employees/capabilities/share-files-in-chat) when the employee should deliver a file to the contact.
