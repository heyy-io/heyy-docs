**Assets** are images, videos, documents, and audio your employee can **send to contacts** during a conversation — brochures, product photos, PDFs, and similar shareable files.

This is different from **Content**, which is internal knowledge used to generate answers but never sent to contacts.

<Warning>
Only upload files you are willing to send to customers. Do not add internal or sensitive reference material here — use **Content** instead.
</Warning>

## Add and manage assets

On the **Assets** tab:

1. Click **Add asset** or drag files onto the page.
2. Choose the file type (image, video, document, or audio).
3. Add a **description** — when to send this file and what the contact should understand.
4. Save.

Your plan may limit how many assets you can add per employee. Edit descriptions when use cases change; delete assets you no longer want the employee to offer.

Test delivery in the [Playground](/help/ai-employees/going-live/test-in-playground) after adding new assets. Reference assets in [guidelines](/help/ai-employees/capabilities/reference-assets-in-guidelines).
