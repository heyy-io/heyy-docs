---
title: "Let your employee update contact fields"
description: "Allow the AI to write contact data during conversations."
---

On **Attributes**:

1. Add an attribute.
2. Enable **Update** (with or without read).

Use updates for capture flows (email, preferences, lead stage). Pair with [Boundaries](/help/ai-employees/identity-and-guidelines/boundaries) so the AI confirms before changing sensitive data.
