---
title: "Trigger an automation"
description: "Let your employee start another workflow for the contact."
---

To add a **Trigger automation** action on the **Actions** tab:

1. Click **Add action** and choose **Trigger automation**.
2. Select the automation the employee should start.
3. Save.

The employee can invoke this action when a conversation needs a side effect your main automation does not handle inline — for example, creating a follow-up journey, tagging the contact, or kicking off a fulfillment workflow.

Test the trigger path in the [Playground](/help/ai-employees/going-live/test-in-playground) and confirm the target automation is published and appropriate for the channel.
