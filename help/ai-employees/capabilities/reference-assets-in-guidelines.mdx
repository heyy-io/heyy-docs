---
title: "Reference assets in guidelines"
description: "Tell the employee when to send specific files."
---

In **Identity & guidelines**, use [Conversation flow & objectives](/help/ai-employees/identity-and-guidelines/conversation-flow-and-objectives) guidelines to explain when each asset should go out — for example, send the pricing PDF after qualification, or the setup guide when onboarding starts.

Type `@` in the guideline editor and pick **Assets** to reference configured files. Mentions tie prompts to what you uploaded on the **Assets** tab.

Pair asset rules with [Boundaries](/help/ai-employees/identity-and-guidelines/boundaries) so the employee does not overshare files or send the wrong document.
