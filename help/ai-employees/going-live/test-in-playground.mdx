---
title: "Test in playground"
description: "Validate behavior before customers see your employee."
---

The **Playground** is a side panel on every AI Employee page. Use it to chat with the employee using its live configuration.

From the toolbar:

- Open or close the Playground with the **Playground** button.
- Send messages as if you were a contact.
- Observe replies, [actions](/help/ai-employees/capabilities/actions-overview), asset delivery, and [exit points](/help/ai-employees/capabilities/create-exit-points).

When an exit point triggers, the Playground shows that the route fired. Click **Reset chat** to start a fresh test session.

Run through your most common customer questions and edge cases before [adding the employee to an automation](/help/ai-employees/going-live/add-to-automation).
