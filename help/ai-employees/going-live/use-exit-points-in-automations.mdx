---
title: "Use exit points in automations"
description: "Branch your workflow when an exit point fires."
---

After you [create exit points](/help/ai-employees/capabilities/create-exit-points) on the employee, map them in the automation **AI Employee** action:

1. Open [**Automations**](https://app.heyy.io/automations) and edit the workflow.
2. Select the **AI Employee** node.
3. Connect each exit point to the next step — assign chat, send a message, close the chat, or another action.

Common mappings:

- **Request human** → Assign to team or move to human inbox
- **Issue resolved** → Close chat or send satisfaction follow-up
- **Needs specialist** → Tag contact and notify a teammate

See [Add an AI employee to an automation](/help/ai-employees/going-live/add-to-automation) for the full setup flow.
