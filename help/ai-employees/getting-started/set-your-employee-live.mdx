---
title: "Set your employee live"
description: "Activate an AI Employee so it can respond in production."
---

An AI Employee must be **live** (active) before it can handle conversations in automations.

1. Open [**AI Employees**](https://app.heyy.io/ai-employees).
2. Select the employee from the sidebar.
3. On the employee card or overview, choose **Set live**.

To pause the employee without deleting it, use **Set inactive**. Inactive employees keep their configuration but do not run in automations.
