---
title: "Hire an AI employee"
description: "Create your first AI Employee in Heyy."
---

To hire an AI employee:

1. Open [**AI Employees**](https://app.heyy.io/ai-employees).
2. Click **Hire AI Employee** (or choose **+** in the employee selector).
3. Enter a name and complete the setup flow.

You are redirected to **Identity & guidelines** to finish configuration. Your plan may limit how many employees you can create.

Next: [Identity setup](/help/ai-employees/identity-and-guidelines/identity-setup), then [run your first test conversation](/help/ai-employees/getting-started/run-your-first-test-conversation).
