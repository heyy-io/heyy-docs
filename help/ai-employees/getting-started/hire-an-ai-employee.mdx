**Hiring** an AI Employee creates a new employee record in your workspace — a container for the identity, knowledge, and capabilities you configure on the following pages. It takes under a minute; everything is pre-filled with a sensible default, so you can refine it after.

<Tip>
  A new AI Employee is **inactive** by default. It will not appear as an option inside [automations](/help/automations/index) until you [set it live](/help/ai-employees/going-live/test-in-playground), which should only happen after testing in the [Playground](/help/ai-employees/going-live/test-in-playground).
</Tip>

## Open AI Employees

Go to **AI Employees** in the top navigation bar. Your employees list on the left; selecting one opens their card, and the workspace updates to show that employee's name, role, avatar, and tabs.

<Frame>
  ![AI Employees list](/assets/ai-employees-list.png)
</Frame>

If you have no employees yet, you land in an empty state with a single **Hire AI Employee** button.

## Fill out the hire form

Clicking **Hire AI Employee** opens a short form. Every field is pre-filled with a default, so you can accept it and refine later, or set it deliberately now.

<Frame>
  ![Hire an AI Employee form](/assets/ai-employees-hire-form.png)
</Frame>

| Field | What it does |
| ----- | ------------ |
| **Name** | The employee's display name. It introduces itself using this name, and the name is translated automatically in multilingual conversations. |
| **Gender** | Mostly relevant for gender-specific languages such as Hebrew or Arabic. Choose **AI Employee** (neutral) if you do not want it to identify as male or female. |
| **Role** | **Support**, **Sales**, or **General**. Pre-fills the employee's behind-the-scenes instructions and tone, so pick the closest match to the job it will do. |

Click **Hire** to create the employee. You are redirected to **Identity & guidelines** to continue setup — see [Identity setup](/help/ai-employees/identity-and-guidelines/identity-setup) for how Name, Gender, and Role are used there.

## Plan limits

Your plan sets how many AI Employees you can hire, and what each one can hold. If you hit a limit, you are prompted to upgrade before continuing.

<Frame>
  ![AI Employee limits by plan](/assets/ai-employees-plan-limits.png)
</Frame>

| Plan | AI Employees | Knowledge per employee | Actions per employee | AI Assets per employee |
| ---- | ------------ | ----------------------- | --------------------- | ------------------------ |
| Hobby | 1 | 3 MB | 3 | — |
| Pro | 2 | 20 MB | 8 | 50 |
| Ultra | 3 | 50 MB | 12 | 500 |
| Enterprise | Higher limits | Higher limits | Higher limits | Higher limits |

Knowledge is measured by extracted text, not file size — see [Keep knowledge accurate](/help/ai-employees/content/keep-knowledge-accurate). Hitting the employee limit does not affect existing employees; it only blocks hiring another until you upgrade or retire one. Enterprise limits are set per account — contact sales if you are scaling past Ultra.
