---
title: "Run your first test conversation"
description: "Validate replies in the Playground before going live."
---

Before customers interact with your employee, run a test in the **Playground**:

1. Open an AI Employee in [**AI Employees**](https://app.heyy.io/ai-employees).
2. Open the **Playground** panel from the toolbar on the right.
3. Send messages and review replies.

The Playground uses the employee's current configuration. If an exit point triggers, reset the chat to keep testing.

See [Test in the Playground](/help/ai-employees/going-live/test-in-playground) for attachments, actions, and troubleshooting.
