---
title: "Run your first test conversation"
description: "Validate replies in the Playground before going live."
---

Before customers interact with your employee, run a test in the **Playground**:

1. Open an AI Employee in [**AI Employees**](https://app.heyy.io/ai-employees).
2. Open the **Playground** panel (toolbar on the right).
3. Pick a [test contact](/help/ai-employees/testing-and-quality/simulate-real-contact) or use the default session.
4. Send messages and review replies.

See [Test in the Playground](/help/ai-employees/testing-and-quality/test-in-playground) for attachments, actions, and troubleshooting.
