---
title: "Connect to real customer traffic"
description: "Wire an AI Employee into channels via automations."
---

AI Employees reach customers through **automations**, not by sitting on a channel alone.

1. Finish configuration — start with [Identity setup](/help/ai-employees/identity-and-guidelines/identity-setup) and [Content](/help/ai-employees/content/add-your-website) as needed.
2. [Test in the Playground](/help/ai-employees/going-live/test-in-playground).
3. [Add the employee to an automation](/help/ai-employees/going-live/add-to-automation) on the channel you use (WhatsApp, Instagram, Live Chat, etc.).
4. Publish the automation.

When a trigger fires (for example, a new incoming message), the automation can hand the conversation to your AI Employee. See [How AI employees work in automations](/help/ai-employees/going-live/how-ai-employees-work-in-automations) and [Messaging channels](/help/messaging-channels/whatsapp/getting-started) if you have not set up a channel yet.
