---
title: "Connect to real customer traffic"
description: "Wire an AI Employee into channels via automations."
---

AI Employees reach customers through **automations**, not by sitting on a channel alone.

1. [Set your employee live](/help/ai-employees/getting-started/set-your-employee-live).
2. [Add the employee to an automation](/help/ai-employees/going-live/add-to-automation) on the channel you use (WhatsApp, Instagram, Live Chat, etc.).
3. Publish the automation.

When a trigger fires (for example, a new incoming message), the automation can hand the conversation to your AI Employee. See [Messaging channels](/help/messaging-channels/whatsapp/getting-started) if you have not set up a channel yet.
