---
title: "Hand off to a human teammate"
description: "Move from AI to your inbox team."
---

Combine:

- [Exit conditions](/help/ai-employees/going-live/exit-conditions) that detect handoff intent
- [Boundaries](/help/ai-employees/identity-and-guidelines/set-boundaries-and-escalation) telling the AI to qualify before escalating
- Inbox routing ([team inboxes](/help/inbox/find-and-organize/team-and-user-inboxes), [assign chat](/help/automations/chats-actions) automation actions)

After handoff, humans work in the [inbox](/help/inbox/inbox-tour) with full conversation history.
