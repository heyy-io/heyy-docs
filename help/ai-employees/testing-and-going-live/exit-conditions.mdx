---
title: "Set exit conditions"
description: "Define when the AI step should end."
---

**Exit conditions** stop the AI Employee step — for example when the customer asks for a human or a phrase you define.

Add conditions in the automation **AI Employee** node. Each condition can branch the flow (handoff, close, another action). Align wording with [hand off to a human](/help/ai-employees/going-live/hand-off-to-human).
