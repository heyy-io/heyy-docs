---
title: "How to invite partners"
description: "Bring in an external collaborator without giving them full workspace access."
---

Choose **Partner** for someone outside your team who needs to work with you in Heyy without full access to your workspace.

## Invite a partner

Still on the **Team** page, click **Invite partner** in the top right corner. The flow works the same way as inviting a member: type their email, hit **Add**, and click **Send invites**.

<Note>
  Inviting someone who's part of your own team instead? See [How to invite teammates](/help/managing-your-workspace/workspace-members/how-to-invite-teammates) and [Workspace roles](/help/managing-your-workspace/workspace-roles) for what each role can access.
</Note>
