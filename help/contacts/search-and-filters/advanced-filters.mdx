---
title: "Advanced filters"
description: "Filter the Contacts table by fields, labels, and attributes."
---

**Advanced filters** narrow the Contacts table to contacts that match conditions you define—before you save the setup as a [saved contact view](/help/contacts/search-and-filters/saved-contact-views).

## Build a filter

1. Open [**Contacts**](https://app.heyy.io/contacts).
2. Open the filter builder (filter icon or filter panel on the table).
3. Add conditions on **built-in fields**, [labels](/help/contacts/mini-crm/labels), and [attributes](/help/contacts/mini-crm/custom-attributes-as-columns).
4. Apply the filter to update the table.

Filtered results reflect **contact** data workspace-wide—the same records used in automations and journeys.

## Save as a view

When you want to reuse a filter, save it as a contact view. Views can also be managed from [**Settings → Views**](https://app.heyy.io/settings/views) where your plan allows.

<Note>
  To filter **chats** in the Inbox sidebar (not the contact table), use [Saved inbox views](/help/inbox/find-and-organize/saved-inbox-views).
</Note>
