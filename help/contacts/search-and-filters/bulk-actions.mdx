---
title: "Bulk actions"
description: "Apply actions to multiple contacts at once on the Contacts page."
---

On [**Contacts**](https://app.heyy.io/contacts), select **multiple contacts** and use the bulk action bar to update many records in one step—instead of editing one row at a time.

Typical bulk operations include updating [labels](/help/contacts/mini-crm/labels), [attributes](/help/contacts/mini-crm/custom-attributes-as-columns), and other contact-level changes your workspace supports.

1. Open [**Contacts**](https://app.heyy.io/contacts).
2. Select the contacts you want (checkboxes on rows).
3. Choose an action from the **bulk action bar**.

Bulk actions run on **contacts**, the same foundation automations and journeys use. For bulk work on **chats**, see [Bulk actions](/help/inbox/find-and-organize/bulk-actions) in the Inbox.

Campaign execution may offer separate bulk updates on campaign results—see [Export & bulk actions](/help/campaigns-and-outreach/run/export-and-bulk-actions).
