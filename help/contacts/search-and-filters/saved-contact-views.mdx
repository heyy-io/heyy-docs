---
title: "Saved contact views"
description: "Save filtered segments of your Contacts table."
---

**Contact views** are saved filters on the [**Contacts**](https://app.heyy.io/contacts) table—segments such as open leads, VIP customers, or everyone with a given label.

## Create or use a view

1. Open [**Contacts**](https://app.heyy.io/contacts).
2. In the sidebar, select an existing view or create a new one.
3. Configure filters with [Advanced filters](/help/contacts/search-and-filters/advanced-filters) (contact fields, [labels](/help/contacts/mini-crm/labels), [attributes](/help/contacts/mini-crm/custom-attributes-as-columns)).

Contact views use the `contact` view type in Heyy. Your plan may limit how many saved views you can create.

## Manage views in Settings

Depending on your workflow, views may also be edited from [**Settings → Views**](https://app.heyy.io/settings/views).

<Note>
  **Inbox views** filter which **chats** appear in the Inbox list—they do not replace contact views. See [Saved inbox views](/help/inbox/find-and-organize/saved-inbox-views).
</Note>
