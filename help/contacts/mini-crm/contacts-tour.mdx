The [**Contacts**](https://app.heyy.io/contacts) page is your workspace contact table—a mini-CRM built on the same records that power Inbox, automations, and journeys.

## Main areas

1. **Views** — Saved segments in the sidebar (see [Saved contact views](/help/contacts/search-and-filters/saved-contact-views)).
2. **Contact table** — Sortable list of contacts with configurable columns.
3. **Contact profile** — Open a row to see and edit the full record (fields, labels, attributes, related chats).

## What you can do here

- Scan and sort your customer base
- Adjust [columns and visible fields](/help/contacts/mini-crm/column-order-and-visible-fields)
- Show [custom attributes](/help/contacts/mini-crm/custom-attributes-as-columns) and [labels](/help/contacts/mini-crm/labels) on the table
- [Search and filter](/help/contacts/search-and-filters/search-contacts) or switch to a saved view
- [Bulk actions](/help/contacts/search-and-filters/bulk-actions) on multiple rows

For messaging a contact, open their chat from the profile or go to [**Inbox**](https://app.heyy.io/inbox). The underlying record is always the same [contact](/help/contacts/the-contact/what-is-a-contact).
