After a campaign runs, the **execution page** is where you review outcomes and clean up at scale.

## Export results

**Export** campaign execution data for reporting, sharing with your team, or analysis in spreadsheets and other tools—without copying numbers manually.

Use exports to answer questions such as who was delivered, who failed, and who still needs a follow-up.

## Bulk actions on results

From the execution page you can:

- Select multiple recipients and apply **bulk actions** (for example update [contact attributes](/help/contacts/mini-crm/custom-attributes-as-columns) for everyone who failed or everyone who engaged)
- Start a **new campaign** in a few clicks—especially useful for a follow-up to contacts with **failed** messages after you fix the template or channel

Bulk updates run on **contacts**, consistent with the [Contacts](/help/contacts/mini-crm/contacts-tour) mini-CRM and [automations](/help/automations/contacts-actions).
